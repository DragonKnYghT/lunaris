How to Play

Getting Started

Welcome to Lunaris! This guide will help you understand the basics of the game.

Game Modes

Roguelike Mode

Embark on an adventure through procedurally generated zones. Each run is unique as you face random encounters, collect creatures, and try to reach the end.

Versus Mode

Battle against other players in real-time matches. Build your team, strategize, and climb the ranks!

Combat Basics

Lunaris features turn-based combat where creatures take turns using moves, items, or switching to another creature.

Type Advantages

Each creature has one or two types. Certain types have advantages over others. Use type matchups to gain the upper hand!

Abilities

Creatures can have unique abilities that provide passive bonuses or activate under certain conditions.

Collecting Creatures

Throughout your adventure, you'll encounter wild creatures. Capture them using special items to add them to your team!

Tips for Beginners

  • Pay attention to type advantages in battle
  • Build a balanced team with different types
  • Don't forget to use items during battle
  • Save your progress regularly
  • Experiment with different strategies
